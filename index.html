Welcome

Rooted in service since 1903

Founded by Mother Mary Kevin Kearney, St. Francis Hospital Nsambya is a private not-for-profit hospital owned by Kampala Archdiocese, with a heritage associated with the Little Sisters of St. Francis.

Its mission is to provide quality and sustainable healthcare, training and research with consideration for underserved communities.
