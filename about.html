About the Hospital

Our origins, Franciscan heritage, mission, vision and institutional leadership.

Our history

Founded in 1903

St. Francis Hospital Nsambya was founded in 1903 by Mother Mary Kevin Kearney. Rooted in the Franciscan tradition, the hospital was entrusted to the Little Sisters of St. Francis and is owned by Kampala Archdiocese.

The hospital operates as a private not-for-profit institution. Its work brings together healthcare, training and research, with consideration for underserved communities.

Franciscan heritage

The hospital's heritage is associated with the Little Sisters of St. Francis.

Education and training

The hospital's training school developed nursing, midwifery and laboratory education. Its published history records midwifery training from 1921.

Today

The hospital continues to describe its work through healthcare, training and research in the spirit of Love & Serve.
